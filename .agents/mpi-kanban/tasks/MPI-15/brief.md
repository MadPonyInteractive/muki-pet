# MPI-15 Headset when remote control is on

Fabio's idea (2026-11-22). While Remote Control is on, Muki wears a one-ear headset:

- a thin band over the top of his screen head;
- one round earpiece on the side of the head;
- a small boom mic curving from the earpiece toward the bottom corner of his face;
- a short antenna on the earpiece, its tip blinking slowly and softly ("connected").

It drops onto his head when Remote Control turns on (he does a quick happy squint) and lifts off when it ends.
One earpiece, not a full two-cup headset: his head is small in the band and two cups look heavy.

Test (Fabio): `/reload-plugins`, then turn Remote Control on and off.
