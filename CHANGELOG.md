# Changelog

What changed in each version of Muki. The newest version comes first, and its number matches `version` in `muki-pet/.claude-plugin/plugin.json`.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-23)

The first version.

- Muki lives in a band above the chat box in Claude Code Desktop. He wanders, his head lags behind on a spring, he watches your mouse, blinks, and falls asleep after a quiet spell.
- He reacts to what Claude is doing: alert when you send a message, focused while Claude works, happy at an answer, sad at a failure, curious when a tool needs your OK.
- He visits a prop for each kind of tool: a file, a magnifying glass, a pencil, a terminal or a globe.
- Background tasks pile up as papers at the end of his road, and he rolls his eyes at them.
- He heats up as the context window fills: warm from 20%, red and sweating from 30%, suffering from 40%. From 30% he's tired and slow.
- He wants attention: lonely after 10 minutes without play, mad after 20, tantrums from 25. A pet or a cookie calms him at once.
- Play: pet him, boop him, feed him cookies. Six in a row makes him sick.
- He celebrates with fireworks when you tell Claude it worked.
- He wears a headset while you message the session from your phone or the web.
- With reduced motion turned on, he stays at home and doesn't roll.
- No chat, no model or network calls, no credits used.
