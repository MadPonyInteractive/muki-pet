## Middle click mostly acted like a left click (MPI-23)

- **Fabio, live:** a middle click mostly gave food, like a left click; once the toy flashed for a moment, then food showed under the pointer.
- **Cause, reproduced in headless Chrome with real presses (DevTools `Input.dispatchMouseEvent`, button middle):** on a page that can't scroll, 21 of 21 middle clicks gave a toy, also in a `sandbox=""` frame like Desktop's. Add 3000 px below the band so the page can scroll, and 0 of 8: Chromium's middle-button autoscroll starts (it looks past the frame for anything scrollable, the chat in Desktop), and then no `auxclick` comes at all. `mousedown`, the focus and `mouseup` still come, so the press focused a cookie strip and he went for the food. Desktop is Chromium 156 (Electron 45.3.3), installed Chrome 158: same rules. The frame can't stop autoscroll (no script, and the scrub removes `<a>`, which is where Chromium skips it).
- **Fix:** the switches start on `mouseup` (any button) and end on `click+.001s` (left only). Tried first: ending on `click` with no offset. An end at the same instant as the begin is ignored, so every left click gave a toy. With the 1 ms: left clicks show no toy in any frame (counted every animation frame) and keep the cookie focus; middle gives a toy 10 of 10 on the scrollable page, 6 of 6 on the plain one; middle again swaps; right still works; a petting drag let go outside the frame starts nothing. Same results in the lab's sandboxed frame on a scrollable page.
- **Left over (Chromium, can't be changed from the frame):** with autoscroll on, a quick middle click leaves autoscroll running, and the next left click only stops it. It arrives as a release with no `click`, so it swaps the toy instead of putting it away; a second left click puts it away. Same with the bed: the first left click is eaten. Not seen live: whether the chat scrolls as the mouse moves after a middle click on him.
