- Fabio: he almost never saw Muki lonely (sad), only mad; soon after a pet or a cookie he was mad again, and tantrums never ended however much he petted him or fed him cookies.
- First suspect (from the handoff): the band's `near` events don't arrive while the mouse is inside his interactive frame. - **Cause, read in Desktop's renderer (`ion-dist.asar`, Claude 6.28899, the Client host near "changed its own size") and in the mod types:** "A `setState` on each of three renders in a row with no key, pointer, tick, press or props between is a render loop: the instance unmounts." A resize is none of those, and the sensor called `setState` on every change (wake, over, left), so its third change unmounted it. Desktop retries a faulted Client on later band updates (up to 3 times), which is the fresh instance seen at a turn start.
- **Fix:** `band/sensor.ts` keeps what it has seen in a module variable, not `surface.state`, and never calls `setState`. Test: "the sensor reports every visit of your mouse and never calls setState" (`register.test.tsx`) calls the module directly with a fake surface; it fails on the old sensor and passes on the new. `claude plugin test`'s `ui.resize` does not apply Desktop's render-loop rule (an end-to-end version with six visits passed on the old sensor too), hence the direct test.
