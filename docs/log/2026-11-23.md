## MPI-18: pets and cookies didn't end his mad or tantrum

- Fabio: he almost never saw Muki lonely (sad), only mad; soon after a pet or a cookie he was mad again, and tantrums never ended however much he petted him or fed him cookies.
- First suspect (from the handoff): the band's `near` events don't arrive while the mouse is inside his interactive frame. Old attention logs from 2026-11-22 (`%TEMP%/claude/muki-pet-attention.log.*`) showed that in chats running current code every near span of 1 s or more reset his clock, so the brain's logic was fine. Many chat processes on the PC were days old, still running older mod code (the 5 s play rule), which explained part of it but not all.
- The temporary attention log came back (`brain/debug-log.ts` restored from d559e65) with timers at 1/2/3 min. Test 1 (hover, hold the button, cookie): one 11 s near span, clock reset, so a pressed button does not drop the hover. Test 2: the brain heard nothing from 08:01:39 on: lonely, mad and the tantrum came, Fabio petted him, and no `near` event arrived. The drawing's own hover calm showed (CSS), the clock never reset, so he went straight back to angry.
- Test 3 with every sensor post logged: the sensor posted on wake, over and left, then went silent for good; at a turn's start a brand-new instance (`wide:false`) appeared, posted once and went silent again.
- **Cause, read in Desktop's renderer (`ion-dist.asar`, Claude 6.28899, the Client host near "changed its own size") and in the mod types:** "A `setState` on each of three renders in a row with no key, pointer, tick, press or props between is a render loop: the instance unmounts." A resize is none of those, and the sensor called `setState` on every change (wake, over, left), so its third change unmounted it. Desktop retries a faulted Client on later band updates (up to 3 times), which is the fresh instance seen at a turn start.
- **Fix:** `band/sensor.ts` keeps what it has seen in a module variable, not `surface.state`, and never calls `setState`. Test: "the sensor reports every visit of your mouse and never calls setState" (`register.test.tsx`) calls the module directly with a fake surface; it fails on the old sensor and passes on the new. `claude plugin test`'s `ui.resize` does not apply Desktop's render-loop rule (an end-to-end version with six visits passed on the old sensor too), hence the direct test.
- Live, Fabio after `/reload-plugins` with 1/2/3 min timers: "it looks right now". Log: lonely, mad and tantrum each went back to neutral on a pet, five visits in a row. Temporary log removed, timers back to 10/15/20. Checks: tsc 0; plugin test 55/55; validate passes (usual warnings); golden identical (3,648, widest 98,894).
- Fabio, next: the tantrum's red "!!" becomes a red heart that breaks in the middle, falls to the floor, and he runs it over (card made). He may tune the lonely-to-mad timing later, after living with it.
- Fabio, after the handoff, in a chat not yet reloaded (still on 1/2/3 min timers): a quick cookie did not calm a mad Muki; a long pet did. ## MPI-19: the tantrum's broken heart, and a faster stomp

- The red "!!" became a red heart (draw/features/tantrum.ts): it pops, beats once, cracks along a zigzag, the halves tumble to the floor beside his wheels and each wheel's slams flatten its half; all fade at the burst's end. Timing comes from the stomp's own slam times. Fabio approved the look in the frame lab.
- Live (1/2/3 min timers), Fabio: after a pet he did the tantrum's body motion with no steam or heart, then ghosted to calm. Cause: the hover rules swapped the stomp and head-shake animations out (touch's hover hop, and `animation:none` on the pet tilt) and `display:none` on the steam; an animation swapped out restarts when the hand leaves, so 2 s later the stomp played alone, and the calm drawing (drawn NEAR_GRACE 3 s after the hand leaves) caught him mid-stomp. Fix: under the hand every loop keeps running; `transform:none!important` holds him still and the steam is `visibility:hidden`. Not yet confirmed live.
- Fabio: the side-to-side jumps were too slow; he wants a kid's stamping, one leg then the other, very fast. The stomp is now 34 stamps in the 5 s burst (about 7 a second) on the spot: he rocks on one wheel's foot, lifting the other, and slams it down with a small squash.
- Checks: tsc 0; golden rewritten (every drawing carries the tantrum CSS; widest 103,394); validate passes; plugin test 55/55 with the real timers (3 timing tests fail while 1/2/3 min test timers are in).
- Fabio, live: he wants Muki okay at once after a pet or a cookie, not 3 s after (NEAR_GRACE: the calm drawing waits until the mouse has left the band 3 s, so play is never cut by a redraw). Done in the drawing instead (no redraw from play): a custom property `--rage` (1) on `.bd` scales every tantrum move and mark (stomp lift and squash, head shake, steam and heart opacity); an animation `rage` (PLAY_MS, step-end, forwards) runs only while `.bot:hover` or `.mv:focus-within` (a cookie or a boop) and flips it to 0 for the rest of the drawing. Proven in the frame lab with a real hover: after 1 s on him and leaving, --rage 0, stomp and shake identity, steam opacity 0. He still stays on his spot until the calm drawing comes (a still mood has no wandering route). The mad/lonely face was already plain after a pet for GLOW (15 s).
- Tried first: `@property --rage{syntax:'<number>'...}`. Desktop's sanitizer (DOMPurify) strips `<number>` from the style text as if it were a tag, leaving `syntax:''`, so the property never registered and every `var(--rage)` went invalid (the stomp became `none`). Unregistered custom properties animate as a flip, which is all a latch needs.
- Fabio, live: a tantrum burst played at the same time as the paper-pile trip.
